HALF
THE ROAD

The passion, pitfalls & power of women's professional cycling.

DOCUMENTARY · 2014 · 106 MINUTES

A story about equality, determination, and change.

Directed by filmmaker and professional cyclist Kathryn Bertine, Half the Road explores the world of women's professional cycling and the barriers faced by female athletes in a male-dominated sport.

Through the voices and experiences of athletes, the film examines the fight for opportunity, recognition, and a more equitable future.
